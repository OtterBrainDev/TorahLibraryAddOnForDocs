Searches a word or theme across the corpuses you choose, then inserts a random set of the sources it finds. Leave the word blank and a Hebrew word from Tanakh is picked for you.

Corpuses
How it works
  1. Uses your word, or picks a Hebrew word from Tanakh.
  2. Searches Sefaria in the corpuses you chose.
  3. Shuffles the sources it can insert.
  4. Inserts as many as you asked for at the cursor, with your insertion settings.
