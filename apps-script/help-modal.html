Quick start: insert your first source

  1. Put your cursor where the source should go.
  2. Open Extensions → Torah Library → Texts. The sidebar opens on the right.
  3. Type a reference such as Genesis 1:1, Berakhot 2a or בראשית א:א. Results appear as you type.
  4. Click a result. The Preview shows exactly what will be inserted.
  5. Optional: open 📰 Insertion Options to choose Hebrew, translation or both, and how they sit on the page.
  6. Click Add Source (or press Ctrl/⌘ + Enter).

Find a text (Texts tab)

Type into the search box. The search runs as you type (after two characters), or press Enter. You can search for:

  • a reference — Exodus 20:1-14, Berakhot 2a, Mishnah Avot 1:1
  • a reference in Hebrew — שמות כ:א, ברכות ב.
  • a traditional citation — Hil. Shabbat 1:1, Rambam, Hil. Teshuvah 3:4
  • a book title, a phrase from the text, or a link copied from sefaria.org

If nothing matches, the sidebar suggests related titles instead of leaving the list empty. Click one to search for it.

While the search box is empty, the Texts tab lists your most recent and pinned sources — click one to open it again.

Working with results

A אShow only works that have a translation in a given language. Show all lists every language.
Rel. ▾Sort by relevance, A–Z, Z–A, category, or most recent.
❌ on a groupHides that whole group of results (for example, all commentary). Bring it back with ⤦ Restore Corpus.
↑ ↓Move through the results from the search box; Enter picks the highlighted one.

Once you pick a result, a card shows the selected source. ‹ Prev and Next › step to the neighbouring passage; Open on Sefaria opens it on sefaria.org in a new tab.

Some results are whole books or chapters headings rather than text — for example Genesis on its own. When the search can tell, it leaves them out of the results and says what to add (a chapter, a verse). If you pick one anyway, the sidebar says so, keeps Add Source disabled, and after a moment returns to your results with that entry removed and nothing selected. Add a chapter or verse and search again.

Versions & Preview

Under the selected source, Versions lets you choose which translation to insert (Translation Versions) and, when you need a specific edition, which Hebrew text (Original Versions). Your preferred translation language is set in Preferences → Fonts → Translation; if it isn't available the sidebar tries English, then any other language.

Several translations at once

When the Display is Translation only, or Hebrew and translation in the Stacked layout, each click on a translation adds it to the selection or removes it; no Ctrl or ⌘ needed. Each is inserted as its own block, in the order you picked them, with a blank line between. The Preview can't show several translations at once, so it lists the ones selected, in that order. One translation always stays selected: clicking the last one leaves it selected and says so. Typing in the filter box only narrows the list; a translation it hides stays selected.

Sefaria lists every translation of a book, but some cover only part of it. A translation with no text for this passage is left out of the list. To see those anyway — greyed out and marked unavailable for this ref — turn on Preferences → Search → Advanced → Show unavailable translations. To leave whole languages out of the list and the search language filter, untick them in Preferences → Search → Translation Languages.

Preview

🔁 Preview shows what Add Source will insert right now: the current version, display, layout, vowels, cantillation, transliteration and divine-name replacements. If the Preview looks right, the insertion will too.

Insertion Options (📰)

Click 📰 Insertion Options under Add Source. Everything here applies to this sidebar session only — your saved Preferences don't change unless you click 💾 Save as defaults, at the top of the panel. The panel scrolls; Esc or a click outside it closes it.

The same controls appear in two other places: Preferences → Insertion, where they set your saved defaults, and Link Texts with Sefaria's 📰 Customize this insertion panel, where they apply to the sources inserted in one pass.

Layout

Display picks what is inserted:

A

Translation only.

A + א

Hebrew with translation.

א

Hebrew (original) only.

With Hebrew and translation, Arrangement chooses Stacked (Hebrew above the translation), Right–Left (Hebrew on the right, side by side) or Left–Right (Hebrew on the left). For the two side-by-side layouts, Line by line gives each verse or line its own table row, beside its translation, so the two are easy to compare; off, the whole passage sits in one row.

Under the two pickers, whatever the layout, each a switch:

CitationAdd a short citation of the translation and Hebrew edition used, and their licenses, below the text. Its font and style are in Preferences → Fonts → Citation (8 pt italic, in the font of the text above, unless you change it).
Line by lineShown for Right–Left and Left–Right only; see above.
Line numbersNumber each verse or line in the inserted text. A range that crosses a chapter gets a heading where the new chapter starts.

Insertion Format

The card has a Source section (the Hebrew) and a Translation section. Each is folded to one line that says what is set — for example Vowels, Cantillation, Punctuation · Translit; click it to open the live sample and its settings. Each setting is one row, its name on the left and its control on the right: a switch, a font button, or a choice of two or three. With Display set to the translation alone the Source section is hidden, and with the Hebrew alone the Translation section.

Link title to SefariaOne in each section: link the source (Hebrew) title, and the translation title, to the passage on sefaria.org. Each is set on its own.
Title fontOne in each section: the font and size of that title, whether or not it is linked. Colour, bold and the rest are in Preferences → Fonts → Title, under Translation title and Source title. There, Hyperlink formatting override gives a title a different look while it is linked (on by default, which keeps the underlined look linked titles have always had).
Text fontThe font and size of the Hebrew, and of the translation. A font button opens a small panel with Font and Size (a blank size matches the text where you insert); Colour, style and more… there opens Preferences.
VowelsThe vowels (niqqud): Off; Tanakh, kept in Tanakh and removed from every other text (Mishnah, Talmud, the Siddur, commentaries), as the original add-on did; or On, kept wherever Sefaria has them.
CantillationThe cantillation marks (ta'amim): Off; Tanakh, kept in Tanakh and removed from every other text; or Torah, kept only in the Five Books.
TransliterationAdd a transliteration of the Hebrew. While it is on, Style under it picks the scheme — Simple English, Traditional, Modern Israeli, Ashkenazi, Academic, or IPA. It is read from the fully vowelled text Sefaria provides, so it is complete even with Vowels off; with the Hebrew’s Punctuation off it leaves out the same punctuation, and a maqaf becomes a space.
PunctuationOne in each section: keep the punctuation in the Hebrew, and in the translation.
Bold & italicsIn the Translation section: what to do with the translation's own bold and italics. Keep keeps them. Drop discards them, so your font style applies throughout. Only inserts only the words the translation puts in bold or italics and leaves the rest out. It never changes the Hebrew, which is always inserted whole, with its own bold and italics.

Example: the Talmud without the explanation

Sefaria's English Talmud is the Steinsaltz translation. It prints the Talmud's own words in bold and Steinsaltz's explanation in plain text. With Bold & italics set to Only, only the bold translation is inserted. Here is a passage from Berakhot 2a, for example:

Keep

From when does one recite Shema in the evening? From the time when the priests enter to immerse and purify themselves in order to partake of their teruma.

Only

From when does one recite Shema in the evening? From the time when the priests enter to partake of their teruma.

Line markers are kept. A line with no bold or italics at all is left out, but a passage with none anywhere, like the Aramaic original, is inserted whole. Words that are kept stay bold and italic. To make them plain text, use Preferences → Fonts → ✒️ Source Emphasis → Advanced. The same Keep / Discard / Keep only choice, as your default, is in Preferences → Fonts → ✒️ Source Emphasis.

Fonts and cantillation marks

The Text font and Title font buttons pick the fonts. Many Hebrew fonts in Google Docs (David Libre, Frank Ruhl Libre, Heebo and others) have the letters and vowels but no cantillation marks, which Docs would show as empty boxes. So the add-on sets just the marks a font lacks in Noto Serif Hebrew (Noto Sans Hebrew for a sans-serif font); the letters, and every mark the font has, stay in the font you chose. The preview shows the text the same way.

Session

↺ Revert, at the top of the panel, puts everything in it back to your saved Preferences. 💾 Save as defaults makes the current choices your new Preferences. Both are greyed out until something differs from your Preferences.

Vowels, Cantillation, Punctuation and Transliteration change the text Sefaria sends, so after you change one the sidebar fetches the passage again. Meanwhile the Preview says Updating preview… and Add Source waits; it takes a few seconds. A preference saved in the Preferences dialog reaches an open sidebar too, except a setting you changed here for this session, which keeps its session value.

Inserting a source

Add Source inserts at your cursor in the document. The insertion is, in order:

  1. a title (the reference) — linked to Sefaria if Link title to Sefaria is on (the source and translation titles each have their own);
  2. the Hebrew, transliteration and/or translation, arranged by your Display and Arrangement;
  3. a citation of the translation and Hebrew edition, if Citation is on, styled as set in Preferences → Fonts → Citation.

Fonts, sizes and colours come from Preferences → Fonts. By default each is Match the document: inserted text takes the font and size of the text where you're inserting, so it blends in. You can make titles a heading (Heading 1–6) so they appear in the document outline and table of contents.

In a table: with the cursor in a table cell, one language or the Stacked layout goes inside the cell. Right–Left and Left–Right are tables themselves, so they go directly below the table, and the sidebar tells you so. Sources can't go into a header, footer or footnote.

Sefaria's own bold and italics are kept — for example, the Steinsaltz Talmud bolds the Talmud's words to set them apart from the explanation. For the translation, you can discard them, insert only the emphasized words (see Insertion Options), or change how that emphasis looks under Preferences → Fonts → ✒️ Source Emphasis.

Voices (source sheets)

  1. Open the Voices tab (or Extensions → Torah Library → Voices).
  2. Search Sefaria's source sheets by topic, title or text.
  3. Pick a sheet. Under What to insert choose Citation (just the reference), Text (the sheet's text) or Both (the text, its title linked to the sheet on Sefaria), and optionally a Transliteration scheme. The line underneath says what will be inserted.
  4. Click Add Source.

Your usual choices can be saved in Preferences → Insertion → Insertion Defaults → Voices.

Lexicon (dictionary)

  1. Open the Lexicon tab and type a Hebrew or Aramaic word. The ⌨︎ keyboard helps if you don't have Hebrew set up.
  2. Pick an entry. Under What to insert choose Full entry (word, definition and source), Word + ref, or Definition only.
  3. Click Add Source.

Star an entry to keep it under ⭐ Starred entries. Your recent lookups appear as chips under the search box.

Session Library (📜)

Every source you insert is added to the Session Library (the most recent twelve, plus any you pin), so you can find it again. Open it with 📜 at the bottom of the sidebar, or Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + L.

  • Click a source to open it in the sidebar again.
  • 📌 pins a source so it stays at the top and isn't cleared.
  • Filter by Pinned, Recent or a category; search; and sort by category, date added, or A–Z. ⊟ switches to a compact list.
  • Copy References copies every reference as plain text — handy for a bibliography.
  • Clear Recent removes everything except pinned sources; Reset Session removes everything. Both ask first, and removing a single source can be undone.

The Session Library is kept in this browser only. It isn't shared with other computers or collaborators.

Link Texts with Sefaria

Turns the citations already in your document — Genesis 1:1, Berakhot 2a, Rambam, Hil. Teshuvah 3:4 — into links to Sefaria.

  1. Run 🔗 → ס from Quick Actions, or Extensions → Torah Library → Quick Actions → Link Texts with Sefaria.
  2. A dialog shows progress while your document is scanned.
  3. You get a table of what was found. Each row shows the words in your document and the Sefaria source they'll point to. When a citation could mean more than one source, pick the right one from the dropdown — each option shows a short excerpt to help.
  4. Each row has two checkboxes: Link (hyperlink the words) and Insert (insert that source's text — see Where an inserted source goes below). Tick either, both, or neither. The checkbox in a column header sets the whole column.
  5. Optional: open 📰 Customize this insertion below the table to insert these sources differently from your defaults (see below).
  6. Apply. The dialog reports how many were linked, and how many couldn't be.

Where an inserted source goes

A citation on a line of its own — Genesis 1:1, or (Genesis 1:1). — is replaced by the source, titled with the citation as you wrote it and still linked to Sefaria, the same as Insert Source from Selection. A citation inside a sentence stays where it is, and the source goes below its paragraph. To keep every citation, turn off Preferences → Insertion → Insert from Selection, or untick Replace a citation on its own line in the panel below for one pass.

Customize this insertion

Click 📰 Customize this insertion under the review table. It has the same Layout and Insertion Format controls as the sidebar's Insertion Options — Display, Arrangement, Citation, Line numbers, and the folding Source and Translation sections (Link title to Sefaria, fonts, Vowels, Cantillation, Punctuation, Transliteration, Bold & italics) — plus Replace a citation on its own line. It starts on your saved Preferences, marked Your defaults; once you change something it says Customized for this pass.

  • Your choices apply only to the sources ticked Insert in this run. Linking isn't affected.
  • ↺ Revert to defaults puts the panel back to your Preferences.
  • 💾 Save as defaults makes your choices your new Preferences. Nothing is saved unless you click it; while it saves, the dialog shows Saving preferences….

In Just link and report a count mode there's no dialog, so Insert text after linking always uses your Preferences.

Choosing how much it asks

In Preferences → Linking → After linking:

  • Show a summary, and ask about ambiguous citations (recommended) — links the clear matches and only asks about the unclear ones.
  • Show every match for review — lists everything before anything is linked.
  • Just link and report a count — never asks; unclear citations are skipped rather than guessed. Turn on Insert text after linking to insert each linked source as well.

What is sent to Sefaria

Sefaria's linker has to read the text to find citations. With Candidate passages only (the default, in Preferences → Linking → Document scanning), your document is checked on your own computer first and only passages that look like they contain a citation are sent. Whole document sends everything, which can catch unusual citations the local check misses.

Citations that couldn't be matched

When Sefaria's linker can't match a citation, the add-on looks it up again the way the search box does: with consistent punctuation, Hebrew numerals without their marks, and abbreviations such as Hil. written out. A citation found this way is linked like any other, and its row says Found by searching the spelling that worked.

The rest are listed under ▸ Show … citations that couldn't be matched, below the table. Each row shows the words in your document, every spelling that was searched, and why none matched — usually a chapter or page the book doesn't have, a book name Sefaria doesn't know, or ibid. / there / שם, which points back to an earlier citation. Fix the citation in your document and run Link Texts again.

If the report says some citations fell across a gap in the partial scan, switch Document scanning to Whole document and run it again.

Divine names

The add-on can replace names of God as text is inserted — by default, יהוה becomes יי. The rules are in Preferences → Insertion → Divine Name Mappings:

  • Apply Divine-Name Replacements On Insertion turns replacement on or off for new insertions.
  • Each rule has its own switch and a replacement to choose — יהוה (יי, ה', יקוק, השם), יה, אלוהים, and English God (Adonai, HaShem, G-d) — or Other to type your own.

To apply the same rules to text that's already in the document, run ה → ש Transform Divine Names from Quick Actions. If no rules are turned on, it tells you so and changes nothing.

The add-on menu

Everything is under Extensions → Torah Library. Out of the box the menu has: Texts, Voices, Lexicon, Insert Source from Selection, and a Quick Actions submenu (Quick Actions Sidebar, Transform Divine Names, Link Texts with Sefaria, Unlink Sources, Gematriya Count) — then Preferences and Help & Support. In Simple mode it is shorter: Texts, Insert Source from Selection, Transform Divine Names and Link Texts with Sefaria; switching back to Everything restores the menu you had.

To change it, open Preferences → Menu Bar. Use the arrows to reorder items, move them into or out of the Quick Actions submenu, add dividers, or untick an item to hide it (tick it again to bring it back). Preferences and Help & Support always stay at the bottom, so you can't lose your way back. Restore default menu undoes everything.

After saving, reload the document if the menu hasn't changed — Google Docs builds the menu when the document opens.

Troubleshooting

My search finds nothing, or the wrong book

Try the reference the way Sefaria writes it (Mishneh Torah, Repentance 3:4), or click one of the suggested titles. Check that the A א language filter isn't set to a language the work lacks, and that you haven't hidden its group with ❌ — ⤦ restores hidden groups.

Add Source is greyed out

Either nothing is selected yet, or the selection is a whole book or section rather than a passage. Choose a specific chapter, verse or daf.

The Preview says it isn't available

You have more than one translation selected. That's fine — they'll all be inserted. Select just one to see a Preview.

The inserted text doesn't look like the Preview, or like my Preferences

The sidebar uses this session's 📰 Insertion Options choices, which can differ from your saved Preferences. Click ↺ Revert (in Insertion Options) to go back to your Preferences, or 💾 Save as defaults to keep what you have.

The Preview still shows the old text after I changed a setting

Vowels, Cantillation, Punctuation and Transliteration are applied when the passage is fetched, so the sidebar fetches it again; the Preview says Updating preview… meanwhile. Give it a few seconds. Add Source waits for it, so what you insert is what you chose.

A tab, a button or a Preferences tab is missing

You are probably in Simple mode, which hides the result sort, Restore Corpus, the Texts / Voices / Lexicon buttons, some menu items, and the Search, Linking and Experimental tabs of Preferences. Switch to Everything at the top of Preferences, or tick Show advanced settings there.

Cantillation marks look different from the rest of the Hebrew

Your Hebrew font has no cantillation marks, so the add-on sets just those marks in Noto Serif Hebrew (or Noto Sans Hebrew). See Fonts and cantillation marks. To keep every mark in one font, pick a font that has them all, such as Noto Serif Hebrew.

The font or size is wrong

With Match the document, inserted text copies the text at your cursor — so inserting inside a heading gives heading-sized text. Put the cursor in an ordinary paragraph, or pick a specific font and size in Preferences → Fonts.

A name of God was replaced and I didn't want it to be

Turn off Apply Divine-Name Replacements On Insertion, or the individual rule, in Preferences → Insertion → Divine Name Mappings. Use Edit → Undo in Google Docs to reverse a recent insertion.

Link Texts with Sefaria missed some citations

Read the report at the end. Could not be placed — set Preferences → Linking → Document scanning to Whole document and try again. Could not be resolved — Sefaria didn't recognise the citation; writing it in a standard form usually helps. In Just link and report a count mode, ambiguous citations are skipped on purpose.

Link Texts replaced my citation with the source

That's the default for a citation on a line of its own: the inserted source is titled with the same words, so nothing is lost and the reference isn't repeated. Use Edit → Undo to reverse it. To keep citations, turn off Preferences → Insertion → Insert from Selection, or untick Replace a citation on its own line in 📰 Customize this insertion for one pass. A citation inside a sentence is never removed.

My menu changes didn't appear

Reload the document. The menu is built when the document opens.

Surprise Me is missing

It's experimental. Turn on Enable Experimental Features and Surprise Me in Preferences → 🧪 Experimental, then reload the document.

Things look broken or out of date after an update

Close the sidebar and reopen it from Extensions → Torah Library. If that doesn't help, reload the document. Still stuck? See the Support tab.

Support

Something not working? Try the quick fixes first — they solve most problems. If they don't, we'd like to hear about it.

Email support Send feedback

Quick fixes

  1. Close and reopen the sidebar from Extensions → Torah Library. This clears anything stuck in the sidebar.
  2. Reload the document (your browser's refresh button). Needed after an update or after changing the menu.
  3. Revert to your defaults — 📰 Insertion Options → ↺ Revert — if the output doesn't match your Preferences.
  4. Check the Preview before inserting. If the Preview is right but the document isn't, that's a bug — please report it.

Reporting a problem

Email help@the-merkaz.org. These details help us fix it quickly:

  • whether you use Simple or Everything, and where it happened — Texts, Voices, Lexicon, Preferences, Link Texts with Sefaria, or another Quick Action;
  • what you searched for or selected (for example, Berakhot 2a, William Davidson translation);
  • your 📰 Insertion Options settings — Display, Arrangement, and what the Source and Translation sections say is set;
  • what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • whether reopening the sidebar or reloading the document changed anything;
  • the add-on version, shown on the About tab.
A screenshot of the sidebar and the document is the single most useful thing you can send. Please don't send private document text you'd rather we didn't see — a short sample that shows the problem is enough.

Ideas and feedback

Suggestions are welcome. Use Feedback at the bottom of the sidebar, or the feedback form.

Questions about Sefaria's library itself — a missing text, a translation error — are best sent to Sefaria through the Sefaria Help Center.

About

Torah Library brings Sefaria's library into Google Docs: find a source, preview it, and insert it formatted the way you want. It is not an official Sefaria project.

  • Several translations in one go, each in its own block.
  • Sources are credited. Each inserted source gets a small credit line naming its translation and Hebrew edition and their licenses. It is on by default; turn it off in Preferences.
  • Better search. Traditional citations like Hil. Shabbat 1:1 and Hebrew abbreviations like רמב״ם work, and a search that finds nothing suggests related titles.
  • Your own menu. Reorder, hide and group menu items in Preferences → Menu Bar.
  • Divine names: יי is now offered for יהוה and is the default for new users. If you used the add-on before, your existing choices are kept.

The full technical record is in the project's changelog on GitHub.

Your privacy

Your searches and text go only to sefaria.org, and only when you search, insert a source, or run Link Texts with Sefaria. (The add-on’s windows also load a script library, a stylesheet and a font from Google, and images from Sefaria, like any web page; those requests carry none of your data.) Your preferences are stored with your Google account; your Session Library, recent searches and starred Lexicon entries stay in your own browser. The add-on has no account or server of its own and sends your text to no one else. The privacy policy has the details.
