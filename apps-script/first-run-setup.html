“Only in Tanakh” keeps the vowels in Tanakh and leaves them out of the Talmud, commentaries and later texts.

God's name

Many communities avoid writing God's name in full on paper that may be thrown away. Choose what your community does.

Coming from version 1.0: there, God's name went in as Sefaria has it, and Transform Divine Names changed it afterwards. Now what you tick is applied as each source goes in. Untick everything to insert it as before; Transform Divine Names is still in the menu.
