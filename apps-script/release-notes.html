Torah Library for Google Docs — Release Notes

Torah Library helps you find texts in Sefaria’s library and insert them into your document, formatted the way you want. It is an independent add-on built on Sefaria’s public library and API, and is not made by Sefaria.

Version 2.1

This release makes the add-on say what it is doing. “Link Texts with Sefaria” now shows progress while it scans, asks which source you meant when a citation matches more than one, and reports what it could not link instead of staying silent. The review dialog lets you link a citation, insert its text below the paragraph, or both. Linking also stops uploading your whole document by default — only passages that look like they contain a citation are sent. See the privacy policy for exactly what leaves your document and when.

Formatting gained text colour and highlight for every insertion role, and the source’s own bold and italics are now preserved rather than flattened — so the Steinsaltz Talmud keeps the bolding that separates the Talmud’s words from Steinsaltz’s explanation. Search recovers better too: abbreviated citations like “Hil. Shabbat 1:1” resolve, and a search that matches nothing now suggests related titles instead of a blank panel.

  • Arrange the menu: Preferences → Menu Bar lets you reorder the add-on’s menu and choose what goes in Quick Actions.
  • Several translations at once: each selected translation is inserted as its own block; translations with no text for the passage are skipped and marked.
  • Insert Source from Selection replaces the selected citation with the source again. To keep your selection and insert below it instead, change it in Preferences → Insertion.
  • Help, reorganized: Help, Support, and About each have their own tab, with a search box for finding topics.

What you can do with Torah Library

  • Find and insert: search by reference, title, phrase, or Sefaria link, preview the result, and insert it.
  • Choose a translation: pick from the translations Sefaria has for a passage, with clear labels.
  • Lay it out: Hebrew, translation, or both — side by side or stacked.
  • Know what can be inserted: sections that can’t be inserted directly are flagged before you try.
  • Hebrew display: show or hide niqqud and ta’amim.
  • Link your document: turn citations already in your document into Sefaria links.
  • Fonts and sizes: set Hebrew and translation fonts once in Preferences.
  • Divine names: choose how divine names are written in Hebrew and English, on insert or across the whole document.

Smaller improvements

  • Hebrew references are recognized with any style of quotation mark, geresh or gershayim, colon or dash, and spacing.
  • Cleaner translation names, without stray characters.
  • Preferences is a single scrolling page with the Save button always in reach.
  • Optional features stay out of the way unless you turn them on.

About version 2.0

Version 2.0 rebuilt the add-on from the ground up. Some behavior is stricter and more predictable than in earlier versions:

  • Inserted text no longer shows stray HTML tags.
  • Bold and italics in the source are kept when you insert.
  • Verse and line numbers come from Sefaria, so they are correct even for unusual references.
  • Stronger divine-name and Hebrew display preferences.
  • Choose a translation while you preview, when there is more than one.
  • Better search ranking and matching.
  • Inserted titles can keep the wording you typed.

Good to know

  • Preferences set how the add-on starts for you.
  • The sidebar shows your choices for this session.
  • Preview shows exactly what will be inserted.
  • If something looks wrong right after an update, reload the document and reopen the add-on before changing settings.

Coming later

Questions, bug reports, and suggestions

For support, feature requests, or reproducible bug reports, email help@the-merkaz.org. The source code is on GitHub.

The most useful reports include the workspace you were using, what you searched for, the settings you had enabled, what you expected, what happened instead, and whether reopening the add-on or refreshing the document changed the outcome.
