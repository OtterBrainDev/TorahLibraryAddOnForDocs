Sefaria for Google Docs — Release Notes

Sefaria for Google Docs helps you find texts in Sefaria’s library and insert them into your document, formatted the way you want.

Version 2.0

Version 2.0 follows Sefaria for Google Docs 1.0. If you used 1.0, the basics will feel familiar: the same library, a search-and-insert sidebar, and commands with the same names. Help → How to... → Coming from version 1.0 shows where everything went.

  • Find a text: the Texts, Voices (source sheets) and Lexicon (dictionary) tabs search as you type, from a reference in English or Hebrew, a title, a phrase or a Sefaria link. Traditional citations like “Hil. Shabbat 1:1” resolve, near-misses are listed under Close matches, and a search that finds nothing suggests titles.
  • Preview, then insert: clicking a result shows the selected passage with your current text choices. Nothing enters your document until you click Add Source. Changing Insertion Options keeps your selected translation, Hebrew edition and order of multiple translations.
  • Saved defaults: Preferences remembers how you like sources inserted, in every document, and 📰 Insertion Options changes it for this sidebar session. Save as defaults keeps those changes. A short setup picks the basics, and you can choose a Simple or an Everything interface.
  • Clearer Insertion Options: Display and Arrangement now have visible labels. Line by line and Transliteration stay at the top when applicable; Text and notes, References and numbering, and Fonts and appearance fold with summaries. The choices still apply to the sidebar session. Keyboard navigation supports the choices and Style menu.
  • Reliable session choices: Revert, Save as defaults and profile changes wait for pending settings. A failed save pauses insertion with retry/Revert guidance, keeping your choices available.
  • Layout: Hebrew, translation or both; Hebrew on the right, on the left or on top; each verse beside its translation, line by line; line numbers; titles as headings, so they appear in the document outline. Missing chapter fragments keep blank cells without shifting later translations.
  • Hebrew and translation: vowels and cantillation controls; punctuation on or off; transliteration in several styles; fonts that match your document, with colour and highlight. Cantillation marks your font lacks are borrowed from Noto Hebrew instead of showing as empty boxes.
  • Commentary and Notes: Default, Combine and Drop follow the William Davidson English Babylonian Talmud and Silverstein’s Rashi Chumash and Rashi Ketuvim. Other editions keep their words and source emphasis. Notes separately offers Omit, Inline or Separate for marked footnotes in either language. Separate creates a labelled text section, not a Google Docs footnote.
  • The edition you chose: passage retrieval uses Sefaria’s current Texts API. Availability means an edition has some text for the passage, not necessarily every verse. Gaps in an edition stay gaps rather than being filled with another edition’s words under one credit. A missing edition needed for the chosen Display requires another choice. A requested language with no text cannot create an empty block; Both uses an available language. Exact edition titles are preserved in multiple-translation insertion and links. Book names open a bounded first section; check the preview title.
  • Divine names: choose how they are written in Hebrew and English. They are replaced as sources go in, keeping their vowels and cantillation, or in selected text with Transform Divine Names. Clear the selection to use the current tab’s body.
  • Link Texts with Sefaria: shows progress while it scans, asks which source you meant when a citation matches more than one, lets you link a citation, insert its text below the paragraph, or both, and lists what it could not link and why. Only passages that look like they contain a citation are sent to Sefaria. See the privacy policy for exactly what leaves your document and when.
  • Sources are credited: each inserted source gets a small credit line naming its translation and Hebrew edition and their licenses.
  • Several translations: in Translation only or the bilingual Stacked arrangement, click to select one, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on Mac) to add or remove individual translations, or Shift-click for a range. Insertions follow selection order; the preview lists the selected names.
  • More tools: Insert Source from Selection, the Session Library of what you’ve inserted, Unlink Sources and Gematriya Count.
  • Make it yours: arrange the menu and the sidebar, keep profiles for different classes, and share settings with the ⇅ Import/Export button in either interface. Everything can transfer a selected profile without switching; shared settings are optional. The 🖉 button above the profile list renames the active profile. Disabled Voices and Lexicon stay off when changing interfaces.
  • Selection-aware actions: Insert Source from Selection reviews selected citations even in quiet linking mode. Link Texts and Gematriya Count use highlighted text; clear the selection for the whole document body.
  • Familiar setup: the 1.0 choice applies a one-step compatibility preset. Choose Voices and Lexicon in other setup paths; both off means Add Sources with Sefaria. Help opens How to..., Support, About or Re-run Setup Wizard, with a keep/reset choice.

What works differently from Sefaria for Google Docs

  • Divine-name substitution is configurable. The ordinary defaults replace יהוה with יי; the 1.0 compatibility preset turns automatic substitution off. Change it in Preferences → Insertion → Divine Name Mappings.
  • Each source gets a credit line. Turn Citation off in 📰 Insertion Options or Preferences if you credit sources another way.
  • Insert Source from Selection opens review for selected references. Confirm Insert; an own-line citation can be replaced according to Preferences → Insertion, while a citation inside prose stays.
  • Fonts match your document. Sources take the font and size of the text where you insert them, unless you set fonts in Preferences.
  • Notes start on Omit. Marked source footnotes are left out, preserving the previous inserted output. Choose Inline or Separate in Insertion Options → Text and notes (or Layout in Preferences) to include them. Commentary starts on Default and keeps explanation and its markings.

Good to know

  • Preferences set how the add-on starts for you.
  • The sidebar shows your choices for this session.
  • Preview uses the same text, Commentary and Notes rules as insertion. Check generated numbering, fonts and page breaks in the document; several translations are listed by name in the preview.
  • If something looks wrong right after an update, reload the document and reopen the add-on before changing settings.
