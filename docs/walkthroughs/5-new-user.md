# Walkthrough 5 — First five minutes

**Audience:** someone who has never used the add-on. **Time:** 5 minutes.
**You need:** a Google Doc and the add-on installed.

Sefaria for Google Docs puts texts from [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org), in Hebrew,
in translation or both, straight into your Google Doc.

---

## 1. Open it

The first time you open the add-on, a short **setup** asks three questions:
what you'll use it for, how sources should look (with a preview of
Deuteronomy 6:4), and whether you want **Simple** or **Everything**, with
optional Voices and Lexicon switches. Accept the proposed choices with
**Continue**, or choose **Skip for now**. You can change all of it later.

In your document, click **Extensions → Sefaria for Google Docs → Texts** (or
**Add Sources with Sefaria** if both optional tools are off).

A panel opens on the right. That's the sidebar.

## 2. Find a text

Click where you want the text to go in your document.

In the sidebar's search box, type:

```
Genesis 1:1
```

Results appear while you type. Click **Genesis 1:1**.

*You can also type Hebrew (`בראשית א:א`), a Talmud page (`Berakhot 2a`), or
just a few words from a verse.*

## 3. Check it

The **Preview** shows the selected passage with your current text choices.

Want only English, or only Hebrew? Click **📰 Insertion Options** under Add
Source and, under **Display**, choose:

- **Translation** (A) — translation only
- **Both** (A + א) — Hebrew and translation
- **Original** (א) — Hebrew only

The preview changes as you choose. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the panel.

Line by line and Transliteration also stay at the top when they apply. The
other settings are in three folding groups; you can leave them closed.
**Text and notes → Commentary: Default** preserves a
translation's explanation and markings; **Notes: Omit** leaves out marked
source footnotes. For a different edition, open **Versions** beneath the
preview. Changing a layout option keeps that selection.

## 4. Insert it

Click **Add Source**.

The text appears in your document where your cursor was, with its title on
top and a short credit line underneath naming the translation.

**That's it.**

---

## Next steps when you're ready

- **Change the default look:** click **⚙︎** at the bottom of the sidebar
  (Preferences).
- **Link references** to Sefaria: **🏃 → Link Texts with Sefaria**. Highlight
  text to limit the scan; clear the selection for the document body.
- **Re-run setup:** **Help → Re-run Setup Wizard**, then keep or reset active preferences.
- **Share or restore settings:** click **⇅ Import/Export** beside Interface in Preferences. Simple uses current preferences; Everything lets you choose a profile.
- **Get help:** click **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar, or visit
  [Sefaria's Help Center](https://help.sefaria.org/hc/en-us/sections/20235182393244).

## If it doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Sefaria for Google Docs under Extensions | Reload the document. |
| "Sources can't be inserted into a header, footer or footnote" | Click in the main text of your document, then try again. |
| "Inserted below the table" | Your cursor was in a table, and the layout you chose is a table itself. Choose one language or **Stacked** to insert inside the cell. |
| "Couldn't reach Sefaria" | Check your internet connection and try again. |
| **Add Source** is greyed out | Read the notice: wait for *Updating preview…* to finish; if the chosen edition is unavailable, choose another in **Versions**; for a heading with no text, pick a specific passage such as `Genesis 1:1`. |
| A translation leaves out a verse | That edition may not translate the whole passage. The add-on keeps its gaps instead of borrowing another edition's words; choose a fuller edition in **Versions**. |
| You picked **Genesis** and the preview says **Genesis 1** | A whole book opens at its first chapter, and that one chapter is what **Add Source** inserts. Add a chapter or verse for a different passage. |
| Nothing found | Check the spelling, or try the Hebrew name, or a phrase from the text. |
| No Voices or Lexicon tab under the search box | It is switched off. Turn it on in **Preferences → Sidebar**, then **Save**. |
