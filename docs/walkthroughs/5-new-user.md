# Walkthrough 5 — First five minutes

**Audience:** someone who has never used the add-on. **Time:** 5 minutes.
**You need:** a Google Doc and the add-on installed.

Torah Library puts texts from [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org), in Hebrew,
in translation or both, straight into your Google Doc.

> This is not an official Sefaria project.

---

## 1. Open it

In your document, click **Extensions → Torah Library → Texts**.

A panel opens on the right. That's the sidebar.

## 2. Find a text

Click where you want the text to go in your document.

In the sidebar's search box, type:

```
Genesis 1:1
```

Results appear while you type. Click **Genesis 1:1**.

*You can also type Hebrew (`בראשית א:א`), a Talmud page (`Berakhot 2a`), or
just a few words from a verse.*

## 3. Check it

The **Preview** shows exactly what will go into your document.

Want only English, or only Hebrew? Click **📰 Layout** at the bottom and
choose:

- **A** — translation only
- **A + א** — Hebrew and translation
- **א** — Hebrew only

## 4. Insert it

Click **Add Source**.

The text appears in your document where your cursor was, with its title on
top and a short credit line underneath naming the translation.

**That's it.**

---

## Next steps when you're ready

- **Change the default look:** click **⚙︎** at the bottom of the sidebar
  (Preferences).
- **Link the references you've typed** to Sefaria: **🏃 → Link Texts with
  Sefaria**.
- **Get help:** click **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar, or email
  [help@the-merkaz.org](mailto:help@the-merkaz.org).

## If it doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Torah Library under Extensions | Reload the document. |
| "Sources can't be inserted into a header, footer or footnote" | Click in the main text of your document, then try again. |
| "Inserted below the table" | Your cursor was in a table, and the layout you chose is a table itself. Choose one language or **Stacked** to insert inside the cell. |
| "Couldn't reach Sefaria" | Check your internet connection and try again. |
| **Add Source** is greyed out | You picked a whole book or a section heading. Pick a specific passage, like `Genesis 1:1`. |
| Nothing found | Check the spelling, or try the Hebrew name, or a phrase from the text. |
