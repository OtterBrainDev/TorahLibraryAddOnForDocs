# Talmud (Mishnah, Tosefta, Bavli, Yerushalmi) names the Tanakh data does not
# cover, in consonants. One group per line; # starts a comment.
#
# A line ending in * holds names that are also ordinary words (מאיר "gives
# light", ששת "six", אידי in אידי ואידי): they are capitalized only after a
# title (רבי, רבן, רב, מר, ר') or בן / בר. Every other name is capitalized
# anywhere, after up to two prefix letters (ו ב כ ל מ ש ה ד: ואביי, דרבא,
# בפומבדיתא). generate.py moves a name to the title-only list when the Tanakh
# often uses its spelling as a word, and says so: רבה, or the prefixes would
# make Rabbah of הרבה "much" and מרבה "increases".
#
# Names the Tanakh has (משה, יהודה, שמעון, יוחנן, שמואל, יצחק, אליעזר...)
# are capitalized from its data and need no line here.
